In-house cloud

Keep your data in your building.

Public cloud suits some workloads. For steady, predictable ones, owning the hardware often costs less over three to five years and keeps sensitive data under your control. We help you decide which fits, workload by workload.

Public cloudIn-house cloud
Cost modelMonthly, usage-basedUpfront hardware, low running cost
Data locationProvider's regionYour premises
Internet outageSystems unreachableLocal work continues
Best forSpiky or global trafficSteady internal workloads
